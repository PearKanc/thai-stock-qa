## 🏢 Company Context

Welcome to the technical assessment for **Thai Securities Co., Ltd.**, a leading securities brokerage company in Thailand. We're building AI-powered solutions to help our clients make informed investment decisions.

---

## 📋 Assignment Overview

**Time Allocation:** 3-5 hours  
**Difficulty:** Junior-Mid Level  
**AI Assistance:** You may use AI coding assistants

---

## 🎯 The Challenge

Our Research Department produces daily market analysis reports, investment recommendations, and regulatory updates. Currently, our clients and internal staff struggle to quickly find relevant information across these documents.

**Your task:** Build a working prototype of an intelligent Q&A system that allows users to ask questions about Thai securities market information and receive accurate, contextual answers. (No need to be multi-turn chatbot)

---

## 📊 Dataset

You are provided with the following documents in the `/data` folder:

- `market_reports/` - Daily market analysis reports
- `stock_recommendations/` - Stock research and recommendations
- `regulations/` - SEC Thailand regulatory summaries
- `company_profiles/` - Listed company information

---

## ✅ Requirements

### Functional Requirements

1. **Document Ingestion**: The system must be able to process and understand the provided documents
2. **Question Answering**: Users should be able to ask natural language questions and receive relevant answers
3. **Source Attribution**: Each answer must indicate which document(s) the information came from

### Example Queries Your System Should Handle

- "What is the current recommendation for PTT stock?"
- "Which stocks have a 'Buy' rating with target price above 100 THB?"
- "What is the P/E ratio of Bangkok Bank?"
